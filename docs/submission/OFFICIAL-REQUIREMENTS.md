## Contest and deadline

- Contest: "Crypto World's Fair Hackathon", administered by Colosseum Org LLC (rules §2).
- Rules §5: the Contest Period "starts at 6:00am PT on September 14, 2026 and ends at 11:59pm PT on October 12, 2026".
- Rules §6(a): each Member must register on colosseum.com "before 11:59pm PT on October 12, 2026"; §6(b): the team leader uploads the Project Submission before the end of the Entry Period.
- **Deadline in other zones:** 11:59 pm PDT on 12 Oct 2026 = 06:59 UTC on 13 Oct 2026 = 11:59 on 13 Oct 2026 at UTC+05:00. At the time of verification (18:26 UTC) about 12 h 33 min remained.
- Winners announced by December 5, 2026 (rules §5, §13).
- The hackathon page itself states no time of day; the rules PDF is the authority.

## Tracks (rules §14) — not selected, see status

Main awards open to all ecosystems: Grand Champion $30,000, Public Goods $5,000, University $5,000, next 20 teams $15,000 each. FAQ: "open to builders across all blockchain ecosystems, and every submission is eligible for prizes regardless of the crypto infrastructure used."

Ecosystem tracks (each "best products that integrate with" the named chain): Solana, Tempo, Hyperliquid (Hypercore/HyperEVM), Zcash (blockchain or asset) — $100,000 each across 10 products; Ethereum L1, Base, Arbitrum, Robinhood Chain — $25,000 each (5 winners).

- Teams choose one ecosystem and compete for its pool (worldsfair page). The pages do not say whether a testnet deployment counts as "integrating" with a chain, and do not say how a track is selected in the form.
- **This project has no public-chain deployment.** No track can be claimed as met on the evidence we have. The choice of track, or of no track, belongs to the user.

## Submission portal fields (FAQ "required information in the submission portal")

1. Product name and brief description.
2. Blockchains and tools integrated.
3. Team members and their backgrounds (every member must have a Colosseum account; the leader adds them).
4. Team location.
5. Logo or graphic.
6. GitHub repository link. FAQ: "Open-source repositories are encouraged, but private repositories are allowed" if access is granted to hackathon@colosseum.com.
7. Pitch video: "A two-to-three-minute presentation video."
8. Product-demo video: "no more than three minutes explaining how the product works."
9. Go-to-market strategy, demand validation and plans for developing distribution.

## Other rules that affect the submission

- All Content must be in English (rules §12(a)(i)).
- One Team per Entrant and one Project Submission per Team (rules §7; FAQ). A second parallel project (for example the earlier Signor Scam) must not be submitted by the same person.
- Pre-existing work is allowed if disclosed (FAQ: teams "disclose all relevant past development work in the submission form"; products are judged on work done in the window).
- Judging criteria (rules §8): Functionality (quality of the code), Potential Impact, Novelty, UX ("How well does this Project Submission utilize blockchain to create great UX for downstream users?"), Open-source, Business Plan.
- The FAQ describes additional review factors for the product submission: founder-market fit, insight, product and execution, market size, founder communication, viability, traction. For the GitHub repository the FAQ says judges look for evidence that the team did the work itself (rather than a third party) and for significant work done in the window — relevant to disclosing AI-assisted development.
- Rules §9: entrants "inform Administrator of the status and ownership of any open-source or other third party code" used in the submission. Rules §12(b)(iv): permission is needed from every individual who appears in submitted Content (videos).
- Rules §1: the objective is "to leverage public blockchains to build solutions and experiment with blockchain technology". Our public demo uses no blockchain; only the local prototype does. This is a weakness against §1 and the UX criterion and must be stated honestly rather than hidden.
- Eligibility (rules §3): 18 or the age of majority, whichever is older; individuals subject to U.S. or other applicable sanctions (OFAC) or employed by entities under blocking sanctions are excluded; so are employees/contractors/officers of the Administrator or Contest Sponsors and their immediate family;  persons located or ordinarily resident in listed sanctioned countries/regions are excluded (Afghanistan, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Somalia, Syria, Crimea/Sevastopol, Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions, Venezuela, Yemen) and employer/third-party-rights representations apply (§3(c)). **Only the user can confirm their own eligibility.**
- AI tooling: no policy found on the pages or in the rules PDF.
- No requirement found that a project be deployed on mainnet or testnet; nothing found stating that a testnet is not needed either.
